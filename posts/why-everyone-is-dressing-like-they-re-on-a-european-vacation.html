Why Everyone Is Dressing Like They’re on a European Vacation

Jul 30, 2026

Linen, woven bags, oversized sunglasses, and the effortless fantasy shaping summer style.

This summer, fashion seems determined to convince us that everyone is spending August somewhere along the Mediterranean—even when the closest thing to a European vacation is an iced coffee on the walk to work. White cotton dresses, striped maxis, tiny shorts, breezy button-downs, and oversized sunglasses have become the unofficial uniform of the season. The look is polished but relaxed, slightly romantic, and carefully styled to appear as though very little thought went into it.

The appeal is not really about copying what women wear in Italy, France, or Spain. It is about dressing for the version of summer we wish we were having: long lunches, late dinners, beachside afternoons, and nowhere urgent to be. A woven tote suddenly feels more exciting than an everyday handbag. A crisp white shirt looks less like office wear when paired with denim shorts and sandals. Even a simple dress becomes aspirational when styled with dark sunglasses and a barely-there heel.

There is also something refreshing about how uncomplicated these outfits are. Rather than relying on micro-trends, the vacation wardrobe is built around familiar pieces in linen, cotton, silk, and denim. Color appears in warm stripes, tomato red, soft pink, and sun-faded neutrals, while accessories do most of the storytelling. The outfits feel feminine without being overly delicate and elevated without appearing impractical.

Maybe that is why the aesthetic has become so irresistible. It allows us to dress like the day might turn into something more interesting than expected. You may not be boarding a flight to the Amalfi Coast, but the right sunglasses, a woven bag, and a white linen dress can at least make an ordinary afternoon feel a little closer to one.
